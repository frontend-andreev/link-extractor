What it does

Link Extractor is a free Chrome extension that lists the links of the page you are viewing. Click its icon and a side panel shows every link in page order, as a full URL with the link text next to it; repeats are merged into one row with a counter. Search the list, or filter it by domain, type (pages, files, email, phone), internal or external links, and nofollow, sponsored or ugc. Redirect links from Google, Bing or Facebook show the address they lead to. Then copy the URLs or download a CSV for Excel; long lists are not cut, so a page with tens of thousands of links is copied and exported in full. It reads the page in your own browser, as you see it, so links added by JavaScript and pages behind your login are included.

Questions

How do I export all page links to CSV from my browser?

Open the page, click the Link Extractor icon (or press its shortcut, by default Alt+Shift+L, on a Mac Ctrl+Shift+L) and click CSV. The file has one row per link, in page order, with the full URL, the link text, the domain, the type and more.

Will Cyrillic, Arabic or Chinese link text open correctly in Excel?

Yes. The CSV is UTF-8 with a byte order mark, so Excel shows every script correctly; the separator follows the language of Chrome, and if Excel puts everything in one column, choose the other one under CSV separator in Filters & settings. A value that starts with =, +, - or @ gets an apostrophe in front, so that Excel keeps it as text instead of running it as a formula; Excel may show that apostrophe in the cell.

Can it export 10,000+ links without truncation?

Yes. Copying and the CSV file include every link in the list; nothing is cut off. In our tests a page with 10,000 links is listed in about a second, and the list still scrolls and filters without freezing. The panel reads up to 200,000 links from the page and as many from each embedded frame, and tells you if there are more.

How do I copy anchor text and URL pairs so they paste into Excel by columns?

Open the menu next to Copy URLs and choose Table for Excel: the URL, the text, the domain and the type go into separate columns when you paste into Excel or Google Sheets. Text — URL copies one “text — address” pair per line, and URLs copies only the addresses, one per line.

Does it extract mailto and tel links?

Yes. Email (mailto:) and phone (tel:, sms:) links are in the list with their text, copied as they are on the page. To get only them, choose the type Email or Phones in Filters & settings.

Does it work on single-page apps and dynamic sites?

Yes. It reads the page as it is in your tab after its scripts have run, including web components (shadow DOM). If more links load as you scroll or after “Show more”, scroll and press Refresh in the panel; when the app moves to another page of the same site, the list updates by itself.

Does it give full absolute URLs instead of relative paths?

Yes. Every address in the list, the copy and the file is a full URL, resolved the way the browser resolves it, including pages with a <base> tag.

How can I remove UTM and other tracking parameters from extracted URLs?

Turn on Remove utm and other tracking tags in Filters & settings. It removes utm_source, fbclid, gclid and other tracking tags from the list, the copy and the file, and links that differed only by these tags become one.

Does it merge duplicate links?

Yes. The same address appears once, with a counter of how many times it is on the page. Under Duplicates you can also merge links that differ only by the part after # or by a slash at the end.

How do I keep only visible links?

That is the default: links in closed menus and hidden blocks are not in the list. The panel shows how many there are, and +N hidden adds them back.

Can I filter internal and external links, or only PDFs and images?

Yes. Filters & settings has internal or external links, types (pages, files, email, phones), domains and nofollow, sponsored or ugc. The type Files covers PDF, ZIP, images and other files; to narrow it to one kind, type .pdf in the search box.

How do I get a clean list of search result addresses from Google?

On a Google or Bing results page the panel offers Results only: just the results, in order, without ads and without the search engine's own links.

Can I get the links from only part of a page?

Yes. Select that part, right-click it and choose Extract links from the selection: only the links inside the selection are listed.

Do I need to sign up? Does it send my links anywhere?

No account and no sign-in, and it is free, with no paid features and no ads. The list is built in your browser and the extension makes no network requests: your links go only to your clipboard or a file you download. Our website counts installs, low ratings and removals of the extension; the privacy policy says exactly what.

What can't it do?

It works with the tab you have open: it does not crawl a website, follow links or check whether they work (status codes). Chrome does not let extensions read Chrome Web Store pages or chrome:// pages, and links in embedded frames from other sites need your permission; the panel says which part of the page it could not read.
