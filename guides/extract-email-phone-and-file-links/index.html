How to extract email, phone and PDF links from a page

Add to Chrome — it's free

Short answer: open the page, click the Link Extractor icon, open Filters & settings and under Type choose Email, Phones or Files. The list keeps only those links, and Copy URLs or CSV gives you just them.

Who this is for

Anyone collecting contacts from a team page, a directory or a list of suppliers, and anyone who needs every PDF, document or archive linked from a page: reports, price lists, course materials, downloads.

Which links count as what

  • Email: mailto: links.
  • Phones: tel: and sms: links.
  • Files: links whose address ends in a file extension — PDF; Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents; OpenDocument, RTF, TXT, CSV and EPUB; ZIP, RAR, 7z and other archives; images; audio and video; installers and disk images — and any link marked for download on the page.
  • Pages: all other web links.

Nothing selected under Type means all types.

Step by step

  1. Open the page and click the Link Extractor icon (Alt+Shift+L, on a Mac Ctrl+Shift+L).
  2. Open Filters & settings.
  3. Under Type, choose Email, Phones or Files; you can choose more than one.
  4. For one kind of file, also type it in the search box: .pdf keeps only PDFs, .docx only Word documents.
  5. Click Copy URLs, or CSV for a file with the link text and type of each link.

What you get

Email and phone links are copied exactly as they are on the page, for example mailto:sales@example.com or tel:+15551234567, with the link text next to them in the list and the file. Duplicates are merged, so an address that appears in the header and the footer is one row. File links are full URLs you can open or download.

Limits, said plainly

  • Only links. An email address or phone number written as plain text, not as a link, is not in the list. Link Extractor lists links; it does not search the page text.
  • Files are recognised by their address. A download link that ends in something like /download?id=42 has no extension; it is counted as a page unless the page marks it as a download.
  • It does not download the files. You get their addresses; it makes no network requests.
  • The Internal and External filters hide email and phone links, because they have no site.

When another tool is the better choice

  • Contacts across a whole website. Link Extractor does not crawl; it reads the one page you have open.
  • Downloading every file at once. Paste the copied URLs into a download manager.

Questions

Is the email address copied with “mailto:”?

Yes, the link is copied as the page has it, including any ?subject= part. To get bare addresses, remove mailto: with find and replace in your spreadsheet.

Can I get the PDFs linked from a page inside a frame?

Yes, frames from the same site are read along with the page. Frames from other sites need your permission, and the panel says when it could not read one.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no paid features, and the links stay in your browser.
